Restore Purchase

Already bought Deutli Pro and need it back on a new device or after reinstalling? Open Profile (the person icon at the top right) and tap Restore purchase under the full-course card. You’ll also find it on the purchase screens in the Phrases, Practice and Dictionary tabs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock the full course?

Tap any locked lesson, phrase situation or dialogue, or open Profile, and you’ll see the offer for Deutli Pro. It is a single payment, not a subscription, and it unlocks the whole course and iCloud sync.

I bought Deutli Pro but content is still locked

Open Profile and tap Restore purchase. This checks your purchase with Apple again and unlocks the course. If that doesn’t help, please contact us at alex.d.code@icloud.com with your Apple order number.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes — all lessons, exercises, phrases, dialogues and the dictionary are stored on your device, so they work without an internet connection. You only need one to buy or restore Deutli Pro and for iCloud sync.

How do I change the app language?

The app speaks Ukrainian, Russian and English. To switch, go to Profile → App language. The Cyrillic transcription of German words is shown in the Ukrainian and Russian interfaces.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Go to Profile → Appearance and choose Auto (follows your device setting), Light or Dark.

Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only when you tap the microphone button to say a German word, a phrase or a line of a dialogue. The app listens, shows you what it heard, and then discards it — recognition runs on your device, and nothing is recorded or sent to us. If you said no and change your mind, allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Deutli in the iPhone or iPad Settings.

My progress was reset. What happened?

It depends on whether iCloud sync was on. If you have Deutli Pro and sync was on (it is on by default), reinstall the app on a device signed in to the same Apple Account and restore your purchase under Profile → Restore purchase: your lessons, streak and reviews come back from your iCloud.

Without sync, progress exists only on the device, and deleting the app removes it. Unfortunately we can’t restore it for you: we have no server, and your progress never reaches us.

I found an error in a lesson, translation or transcription

We appreciate the feedback! Please email us at alex.d.code@icloud.com with the details of the error (the lesson, phrase or word, what seems incorrect, and what you think the correct version should be).

Can I request new content or features?

Absolutely — we’d love to hear from you. Send your suggestions to alex.d.code@icloud.com.

Contact Us
