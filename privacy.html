Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

Deutli ("the app", "we") is an app for learning German, made for Ukrainian and Russian speakers who live in Germany, Austria or Switzerland. It is available for iPhone and iPad. We respect your privacy, and this policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves your learning progress, your interface language, your theme and your other settings locally on your device, using standard on-device storage. This data is not sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it. Daily reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled by the device itself. Deleting the app removes this data.

If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may back up this local data as part of your device backup. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

iCloud Sync (Deutli Pro)

If you own Deutli Pro, your progress can follow you between your own iPhone and iPad. A copy of the same learning data — lessons and their results, your streak and day history, review schedules for words, phrases and grammar, the dialogues and practice sets you have finished, the exercise you were in the middle of, and your settings (interface language, appearance, haptics and daily goal) — is written to your own private iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. It is readable only by Deutli on devices signed in to that Apple Account.

We never see this data: it does not pass through any server of ours, and no account is created inside the app. Sync is on by default for Deutli Pro on a device signed in to iCloud, and the switch is per device: turn it off under Profile → iCloud sync, and that device keeps everything local. Turning sync off stops the syncing; it does not erase the copy already in your iCloud account.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Where the app offers to listen to you — phrases, dictionary examples, dialogue lines — the audio is not recorded, not stored and not sent to us. The app asks for the microphone and speech recognition only the first time you tap the microphone button, and the feature can simply be left alone. What you said is shown on the screen and then discarded. Listening stops on its own after a short pause, and after 15 seconds at most.

Recognition runs on your device: the app asks iOS to keep it there, and if the device cannot recognise German locally, the feature does not work rather than sending your voice anywhere else.

We Do Not Collect Personal Data

Deutli does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We do not use advertising SDKs, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Usage Statistics and Crash Reports

So that we can tell which parts of the app people actually use and find out when it breaks, Deutli sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to PostHog, our analytics provider. This covers events such as opening the app, the screens you visit, starting, finishing or leaving a lesson, opening a grammar hint, viewing the purchase screen and turning the daily reminder on or off, plus any crash or error the app hits — together with basic technical details such as the app version, device model, operating system and the interface language you chose.

There is no account, and events are tied only to a random identifier created by the app, not to you. We never send your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier, and nothing you type or say. Your learning content stays on your device. The data is processed on servers in the European Union and is used only to improve the app. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Purchases

Deutli offers one optional in-app purchase, Deutli Pro: a single payment, not a subscription, that unlocks the full course and iCloud sync. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and by our purchase-management provider, RevenueCat. To manage your purchase and restore it on your devices, RevenueCat processes a randomly generated anonymous identifier and your purchase and transaction history. This information is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Apple processes your payment in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Children

Deutli is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest version.
