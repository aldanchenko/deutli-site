Everything you need to start speaking German

From the alphabet to the Konjunktiv II — levels A1 and A2.

📚

Structured Lessons

A trial lesson and 23 lessons — from the alphabet, articles and numbers to the Perfekt, the Konjunktiv II and indirect questions.

🧠

Interactive Exercises

466 exercises — multiple choice, word order, fill-in-the-blank and matching pairs — plus 23 practice sets with 266 sentences to go back over what you’ve learnt.

🏛️

Phrases for Life Here

180 phrases in 10 situations: the Bürgeramt, the bank, the doctor, the flat, phone and internet, Kita and school, the post, getting around, the café and meeting people. Practise with flashcards or a quiz.

🎭

Dialogues You Speak

10 scenes — registering your address, viewing a flat, booking the doctor, the first day at nursery. The other person talks; your half is yours to say, with tiles, the keyboard or out loud.

📖

Dictionary & Verbs

605 words — every noun with its article and plural, every word with an example sentence you can hear — plus 120 verbs with conjugation tables, from the Präsens to the Konjunktiv II.

👂

Train Your Ear

35 listening pairs for the differences a Ukrainian or Russian ear tends to miss: long and short vowels, u and ü, o and ö, -e and -er, dem and den.

🌍

3 Interface Languages

Study in Ukrainian, Russian or English. In Ukrainian and Russian, words and phrases come with a Cyrillic transcription that marks the stress and the long vowels.

💡

Built-in Theory

Tap the lightbulb during any exercise to review the grammar rule instantly — without losing your place.

☁️

Your Progress Follows You

Start a lesson on the iPhone, carry on from the same place on the iPad. Sync runs on your own iCloud — no account, no server of ours. Part of Deutli Pro.

🔒
